## Diagnostic question

Does entity-linked action context directly increase the probability of choosing
preventive maintenance on the same decision states, explaining the higher
maintenance cost observed in the entity-conditioned PPO experiment?

## Locked design

- Policy pairs:
  - shared scorer versus entity scorer;
  - shared scorer with entropy versus entity scorer with entropy.
- Training seeds: 10000–14000.
- Diagnostic environment seeds: 43000–43099, unused by earlier experiments.
- Each policy pair is evaluated on identical states under two state
  distributions: trajectories generated by the shared control and trajectories
  generated by the entity treatment.
- Both policies produce masked action probabilities on every visited state; only
  the designated behavior policy advances the simulator.
- Primary endpoint: entity-minus-shared preventive probability, averaged within
  each independent training seed, separately for both behavior distributions.
- Secondary diagnostics: deterministic preventive take rate, action agreement,
  machine-specific preventive probability, production-risk bins, and behavior
  episode outcomes.
- This is a read-only diagnostic: no training, intervention, checkpoint
  selection, hyperparameter tuning, or evaluation on seeds 50000–50099.

shown with one `tqdm` bar and the runner refuses a non-empty output directory.

## Interpretation boundary

The paired probabilities identify a policy preference on observed states. They
do not prove which internal feature caused the preference or establish
generalization beyond this benchmark instance.
