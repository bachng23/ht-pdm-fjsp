## Question and locked hypotheses
Can an observable, non-oracle joint-assignment heuristic used only during training improve deployed monotonic-QMIX policies on new service configurations and 3–5 machines?
H1: TD+heuristic reduces mean held-out diagnostic rollout cost by at least 5%, with at least 8/10 improving training seeds and paired 95% t CI upper endpoint <0. Nominal guardrail: mean cost increase <=10%. Primary includes N=3,4,5 only, equal weights over N and medium/high contention. H2 (secondary): teacher reduces collisions/waiting and tail cost. Small N=2 TD+oracle is a privileged mechanism control, never part of H1.

## Conditions and information
Per N train one model on a deterministic alternating mixture of two service matrices; evaluate zero shot on three separate matrices (nominal/medium/high). Train separate models at each N=2,3,4,5: this is configuration transfer within size, not transfer to unseen sizes. Fixed K=2. All arms have identical initialization, parameter count, observations, TD budget and monotonic technician-aware mixer. CF=0. Shared feedforward edge-Q trunk receives local observations, stable ID padded to 5, ordered peer slots padded to 4; 80 features with K=2. Padding is zero. Mixer receives original local observations and original queue context. Telemetry required at execution, no assignment resolver there.
Failure law/costs/horizon are fixed within N and identical across train/evaluation configs. N=2: horizon 6, failure_age 3, p=.55; larger N: horizon 12, failure_age 4, p=.55. max_age=8. FIFO queues. All costs retain environment defaults.
Train matrices: A: row i=(2+i%2, 3+(i+1)%2); B: row i=(3+(i+1)%2, 2+i%2).
Development: row i=(2+i%2, 2+(i+1)%2). Test nominal: row i=(1+i%2, 2-i%2); medium: row i=(2,3); high: row i=(3,4). No selecting test cells after scores. Smoke uses horizon 3, failure_age 2 and hidden 16; all sizes/conditions exercised, no scientific gate.

## Teacher and auxiliary objective
Heuristic receives raw current joint observations, masks and known static environment parameters only. It never reads hidden state, simulator transitions, future events or oracle Q. It reconstructs existing queue workload from observed queue positions and service rows. Enumerate at most 3^5 joint candidates, masked, at most one NEW request per technician. Existing queues are permitted. Each machine action score compares expected discounted failure/downtime cost over min(3,remaining horizon), with one scheduled maintenance start after the observed workload, versus defer; charges maintenance and waiting plus a fixed .1*(service_time-1) throughput proxy. This is a fixed short-horizon proxy, not an optimal-policy guarantee. Stable lexicographic tie breaking creates a consistent joint teacher. Train-only masked CE weight=1 on the SAME real replay batch as TD. No enumeration of unvisited states for heuristic supervision.
Small oracle: exact gamma=.95 optimal first joint action for reachable TRAIN-config states, queried only on visited transitions, no evaluation-config labels. Oracle uses privileged future dynamics. Only N=2. Oracle arm has the same model and TD budget. No positive-control learning gate: teacher optimality is audited but neural learning can fail.

## Limits
80-feature padded peer architecture differs from prior 32-feature model, equally in all new arms. Prior results cannot be pooled. Fixed-index roles and full telemetry do not establish permutation invariance or communication efficiency. Per-size models do not demonstrate scalable parameter sharing. Heuristic supervision may be harmful, collision-free labels do not guarantee collision-free neural execution. Development/test service families are small hand-specified panels, not broad distributional robustness. Statistical significance without 5% practical effect fails H1. If TD already near ceiling, retain FAIL and report headroom. Larger cells have no certified optimal floor.
