## Question and frozen intervention
Does the equal-duration corrective/preventive assumption explain the weak production impact, and does correcting that assumption require joint timing or merely an earlier health threshold? Reuse fixed-route preempt/resume six-machine workload, wear/hazard, costs, queues, admission and common wall-tick shocks from production-impact v1. ONLY physical intervention: corrective service started on a failed machine takes ratio × its preventive duration. Ratios [1,2,4]. These are hypothetical sensitivity values, NOT validated plant parameters; positive results alone cannot establish real-world relevance. No scrap, rework, detection delay, new workload, forced stress or altered costs.

Pending queued jobs use current failed status at actual dispatch; a machine failing while queued therefore gets corrective duration. Existing service remaining time immutable, never rescaled when ratio/state changes. Completed-machine and production/maintenance tick semantics unchanged. PM durations and row means matched across capacity/skills; CM means matched at each ratio. Request order and existing FIFO queues unchanged. Failure event still preserves the productive tick and completed work, explicitly isolating duration rather than introducing multiple mechanisms.

## Metrics and gates
Primary cell high/heterogeneous/long64; ratio2 fixed as primary, ratio4 sensitivity only. Physical-mechanism gate: mean instance-relative F ratio2 versus ratio1 makespan increase >=5% AND paired absolute-increase CI lower>0. This tests missing failure-time consequence, not novel coordination need.
Coordination-useful gate at ratio2: mean instance-relative (H-S)/H >=5% AND paired H-S CI lower>0. Retuned H is the primary comparator; never claim success by beating F alone.
Threshold-adequacy diagnostic at ratio2: upper CI95 of (H-S)/S <=2%. This is a predeclared cost-in-time noninferiority margin; not equality/optimality. Report independent gates; do not conjunct them into one forced scientific success. If physical mechanism passes but coordination does not, evidence favors correcting failure physics/health timing over new joint architecture. If none pass, preserve null and inspect assumptions. Secondary ratio/cell/workload CIs descriptive, no familywise claims.
Record direct makespan/ideal, job completions/work conservation, failures, PM/CM counts and service ticks, queue/late queue, failed waiting, productive ticks, maintenance cost, runtime. Duration effect partition: CM_service ticks vs PM_service ticks; no economic superiority claim from makespan only. At ratio1 engineering replay matches old module under identical configurations/seeds/settings (timing excluded); this is implementation consistency, not independent reproduction.
