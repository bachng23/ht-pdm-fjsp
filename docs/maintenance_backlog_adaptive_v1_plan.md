## Question and falsifiable hypotheses
Can a simple health threshold responding to current technician backlog replace the reservation heuristic S? H is a fixed health threshold with risk-aware assignment; A uses the same assignment but requests earlier when the selected technician is busy. S remains the previously implemented heuristic, not an optimal scheduling oracle.
Primary adequacy: at CM/PM=2, high pressure, heterogeneous skills, 64 jobs per machine, upper endpoint of paired 95% bootstrap CI of mean instance-relative (A-S)/S is <=2%. Safety guard: at ratio4 in the same cell, upper CI of relative per-instance nearest-rank p95 makespan (A-S)/S <=5%. Overall adequacy requires both. Failure is inconclusive/inadequate under these margins, not proof that learning or coordination is needed. Fixed-threshold H is an additional comparator, not a positive-effect requirement. No novelty claim.

## Model and policy
Reuse repair v1 production, transition, common wall-clock/machine hazards, resumable jobs, duration/cost settings; no new physics. Ratio1/2/4 are hypothetical service-duration sensitivities, not plant calibration. Healthy queued machines continue producing/aging; failed machines stop. Failed waiting outside a queue is still counted.
For each eligible machine ordered failed-first then safe-start deadline then physical ID, choose technician with the exact H score: duration+current backlog+CM risk of late start. Backlog includes current remaining service, committed FIFO, and requests emitted earlier in this tick. Let b be backlog on that selected technician. A requests when failed or age >= failure_age-min(failure_age-1,offset+ceil(gain*b)). Finishing-stream guard matches H. No forecast calendar, future events or rollouts. When gain=0 A must exactly equal H, including masks/assignment.
Dev grids H offset={0,1,2,4}, A (offset,gain)={0,1,2,4}x{0,1}, S lead={0,2,4,6}. Independently select per ratio on mean normalized makespan equally weighting all four capacity/skill cells and both lengths; ties smallest offset/gain/lead. A has twice H's tuning budget, disclosed. Save/reload frozen policy before evaluation. No training.
