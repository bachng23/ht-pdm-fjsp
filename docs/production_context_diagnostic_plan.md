## Diagnostic question

Why did production-only relational context increase objective variance across
training seeds: feature-scale dominance, over-concentrated action probabilities,
or direct sensitivity of the learned policy to the context input?

## Locked design

- Policies: completed `shared_scorer_entropy` control and
  `production_context_entropy` treatment.
- Training seeds: 10000--14000.
- Fresh diagnostic environment seeds: 45000--45099.
- Both models score every identical state under trajectories generated by both
  the control and treatment policy.
- Concentration diagnostics: normalized entropy, maximum probability, effective
  action count, feasible-logit span, action agreement, and action-kind mass.
- Scale diagnostics: input-vector norms and first-layer preactivation norms for
  base versus context blocks, plus learned first-layer weight RMS.
- Direct context diagnostic: rescore the treatment after replacing only its
  production-context tensor with zeros; report Jensen--Shannon divergence and
  deterministic top-action flips.
- This is read-only: no training, checkpoint selection, retry, hyperparameter
  tuning, or use of reserved test seeds 50000--50099.
- Independent replication unit: PPO training seed. State-level observations are
  descriptive repeated measures, not independent replicates.

## Interpretation boundary

Zeroing context is an internal sensitivity intervention on observed states. It
does not create a separately trained no-context policy and therefore cannot by
itself estimate the performance effect of retraining without context.
